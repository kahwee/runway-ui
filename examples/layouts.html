How these layouts work

Cards share four rows: heading, description, body and footer. Fields share three: label, control and hint. Stat tiles share label, value and detail rows. Each nested component inherits its parent tracks through subgrid, with container queries adapting the number of columns. Native details animates its intrinsic content size where supported.
